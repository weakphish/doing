# What is this?

A simple little TUI to help organize the stack of things you were doing with a tree-branching visual.

Have you ever felt like Hal in [this scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AbSehcT19u0&themeRefresh=1) from Malcom In The Middle?

I often do at work, and this attempts to help with that by letting you keep track of where you are in your work
by modeling it as a stack of related tasks that grows in different branches.

It's less of a TODO App, and more of "what was I doing" app.
